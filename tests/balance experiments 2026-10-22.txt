=== Speed (2026-10-23) ===

Why speed is weak. Speed only sets how often a fighter acts (and how fast it
recovers from stuns and falls); strength sets strike damage AND block power,
agility hit, dodge, block, counter and critical chances.

What one attribute point is worth (run_test_tech_ab.py +ATT, 16,000 mirror
matches per cell, seed 11, margins +-1.5; units: 2 x win rate - 1, in %):

                         lv 1-7   lv 8-14  lv 15-20
  +agility                +36.2     +21.3     +12.7
  +strength               +32.5     +22.3     +13.5
  +health                 +26.6     +13.1      +9.2
  +speed                  +15.9     +12.5     +11.7
  +speed, toughness off   +17.3     +13.1     +10.3

So speed is weakest at low levels, and toughness (the flat damage reduction)
is not the cause. A probe of 3,000 lv 1-7 fights per row, one side given the
point (per fight, treated vs untreated):

                    turns  strikes  damaging strikes  damage dealt
  +1 speed           +26%     +22%              +19%          +17%
  +1 strength         +6%      +7%              +21%          +45%

Extra turns partly go to Guard / Catch Breath / maneuvers, and extra strikes
meet the same blocks. A block subtracts the defender's strength-based block
power; at low levels strikes are barely stronger than blocks, so a strength
point turns many full blocks into damage (and strengthens the fighter's own
blocks).

=== Counters round 2: guaranteed counters for counter techs (2026-10-23) ===

The author's idea (rule_variants.py Cg): a fighter with counter techs counters
every incoming strike that did no damage (fully dodged, fully blocked or
missed), once per counter tech (an advanced one counts as two: Vengeful Fox
counters twice in a row), while it can afford an attack move; such a fighter
no longer rolls the default counter chance. Counters still cost the move's
stamina and qi and no time. Default counters (fighters without counter
techs) are unchanged.

How often it fires (300-600 fights at lv 13-20): a Retaliative Blows owner
counters 0.4 -> 0.9-1.3 times per fight. 90% of its chances become counters
(5% lack stamina/qi for a move at that distance, 3% have no attack move at
that distance, 2% the opponent is already down). For Vengeful Fox, 64% of
its chances give both counters, 22% one, 15% none: the first counter often
uses up the stamina or changes the distance.

A/B (run_test_tech_ab.py, lv 13-20, 16,000 fights per tech, seed 11, +-1.6;
a tech that does nothing scores 0). On this engine the median general tech
scores +8.1:

                                  Retaliative Blows   Vengeful Fox
engine (default counter roll)           +2.2              +2.3
Cg                                      +5.3              +6.9
Cg + half-price counters (Chalf)        +7.5             +10.8

So guaranteed counters more than double the counter techs' value, but leave
Retaliative Blows at about two thirds of an average tech. What holds them
back is the cost, not the frequency: the fight AI mostly guards when it
can't afford an attack, so a counter paid in stamina partly replaces a later
attack instead of adding one (half-price counters, measured before the
author ruled out cheaper counters, reach the median). The author asked for
an attack bonus instead. A defense penalty on the attacker and a hit bonus
on the counter are the same rule (dodge and block chance are the defender's
numbers divided by the attacker's to_hit), so the variants are a hit bonus
(CbH) and a critical-chance bonus (CbK), for counter-tech counters only; the
attacker is already off-balance at the counter (x0.75 defense):
